# Recall Drill

History-taking recall game (Conditions, Presentations and Systems review modes), a React single-page app served under `/recall-drill/` (React Router, `basename="/recall-drill"`). Drills are quizzes stored in the database and loaded from the API (see "Quizzes and playlists" below). `conditions.json`, `presentations.json` and `systems-review.json` are the seed data that `scripts/seed-builtins.mjs` turned into the built-in quizzes; they are no longer bundled or read by the app.

Routes: `/recall-drill` (home: the three default playlists, each with its description and a `done/total` counter, then the Minis section, with History on the right), `/recall-drill/p/<playlistId>` (a playlist: its quizzes under the playlist's section headings, who made it, Random and a Starred filter, and a History panel limited to that playlist; the header title links home), `/recall-drill/login`, `/recall-drill/profile`, `/recall-drill/q/<quizId>` (the drill). Ids are UUIDv7 strings. Old links still work: `/p/cond|pres|sys` redirect to the built-in playlists and `/q/<base64 of "<system>.<name>">` is looked up with `GET /api/quizzes?mode&system&name` and redirected (`lib/legacy.js`, which also holds the three built-in playlist ids, `FEATURED`). An unknown id shows "Drill not found" / "Playlist not found"; a deleted quiz shows "This quiz was deleted by its owner." and can't be played. History and stars are keyed by quiz id (`history.quiz_id`).

Registered in `/vite.config.js` as `recallDrill` -> `recall-drill/index.html`, a thin shell that loads `src/recall-drill/main.jsx`. The Worker serves that shell for every `/recall-drill/*` path that isn't a real file (see Deployment).

Layout: `routes/` (Home, Playlist, Drill, Login, Profile), `components/` (Layout with the account menu, Avatar, Owner, DrillActions, DrillList, History, Minis), `state/` (`AccountContext` user record + saving, `FilterContext` the browsed playlist and starred filter, which decide what Random/Next/New pick from, `useDrillGame` the round logic), `lib/` (`api.js` fetch helper, `content.js` hooks that load playlists and quizzes, `data.js` builds the game's drill from a quiz and holds the modes, `match.js` keyword matching, `legacy.js`, `storage.js`, `auth.js`, `confetti.js`), `recall-drill.css` (plain CSS, not Tailwind).

## How the game works

1. The player picks a playlist (Conditions / Presentations / Systems review), then a drill (random, or from the list, grouped by the playlist's sections).
2. The prompt is shown, and the items appear as numbered face-down tiles. Conditions have five sections: Presentation, Risk factors / etiology, Examination findings, Investigations (tests for diagnosis and monitoring only, with the key finding: specific and high-yield), Medications, treatment, and management (drugs plus the essential non-drug management, e.g. podiatry/offloading, CPAP, pulmonary rehab). Presentations have three: Differentials, Associated features (history), Investigations.
3. The player types one item at a time. Each input is tested against every item's `keywords` regex; a match flips that tile. Matching is live (on input) and on Enter. "New" jumps to another random item.
4. The action buttons sit on two lines: the first has the Reveal button and a square borderless `↺` Drill icon (`.redo.bare`, same height as Reveal; it drills the open section when collapsed, else the last section with missed tiles, and is hidden until something has been revealed, dimmed when nothing is missed), the second has Hint, Back and New. One button (first row) reads "Reveal <next section>" (e.g. "Reveal differentials"); each click reveals that section's missed tiles, and revealing the last one ends the round. Naming every tile in a section by typing fires confetti and moves the button on to the next section. The player can tap a revealed missed tile to credit it. Score is found+credited / total items. The sliding Collapse | Expand toggle by the counter (the highlighted segment is the current state; remembered in localStorage `recallDrillCollapsed`). Expanded (default): every section is open, typing is matched against all tiles, and Reveal goes through the sections in order. Collapsed: one section is open at a time (initially the one Reveal names); typing is matched only against it and Reveal reveals just its missed tiles and leaves it open, but the counter, section counts and score always cover every section. Clicking a collapsed heading switches to it; clicking the open one does nothing. Completing a section never ends the round; it ends when every tile has been named or revealed, and then all sections are shown.

Drill: once a section has missed tiles (revealed, not found, credited or ignored; recalled tiles still count, so a section stays drillable after a successful drill, and starting a drill clears their recalled state), a single square `↺` button (`.redo`) appears in its heading, left of the "X of Y" count. It hides those tiles again so they can be typed; tiles answered become **recalled** (purple, `s.recalled`). Recalled tiles are never put in found/credited, so history, the score and every counter ignore them. While drilling (`s.drill`): only the drilled tiles match; the view is forced to Collapse on that section (the saved preference is untouched); bold blue "Drill mode" text replaces the Collapse | Expand toggle; the page background and grid turn blue (animated, via registered `--bg`/`--grid` and `body.drilling`); the Reveal button becomes a blue "End drill"; the drilled section's `↺` is shown pressed and disabled and other sections hide theirs; the answer box and Hint stay enabled (Hint applies to the drilled section). The drill ends through End drill (`endDrill`), or by itself (with confetti) once every drilled tile is recalled. Unanswered drilled tiles are then shown purple with a dashed border (`s.shown`) and can be drilled again. It also works on a finished round without re-committing it.

## Systems review

`systems-review.json` (mode `sys`, one top-level key `Systems review`) has one drill per system or clinical context (Cardiovascular ... Psychiatric, plus Cancer x4, Post-operative early/late, Pre-operative assessment, Older person, Palliative care, Child, Infection and travel, Pregnancy). Each has `prompt` and one section, `symptoms` (code `S`): `{"<symptom cluster>": [{label, keywords}]}`. Cluster names are free-form per drill (no `categories.json` vocabulary; `check:categories` only checks the shape) and drive the Hint boxes. Drill names must not contain ".". Keywords follow the same rules as below; keep generic single words (pain, fever) off specific tiles, since the earliest/longest match wins.

## JSON format

These files are seed data now (see above); the same shape is a quiz's `data` in the database, which is what `lib/data.js` reads.

`conditions.json`:

```json
{
  "<system>": {
    "<condition>": {
      "prompt": "Symptoms to ask about",
      "presentation":   [ { "label": "...", "keywords": "a|b|c" } ],
      "risks":          [ ... ],
      "examination":    [ ... ],
      "investigations": [ ... ],
      "medications":    [ ... ]
    }
  }
}
```

`presentations.json` has the same shape with a single system (`Presentation`) and the keys `differentials`, `associated`, `investigations`. For broad presentations the Differentials section holds conditions to screen for, risks, complications or injuries (see the `prompt`).

- **Top-level key = system / category** (e.g. `Cardio`, `Resp`, `Renal`, `GI`, `Endo`, `Rheum`, `Neuro`, `Vasc`, `ID`, `Breast`, `Ortho`, `Derm`, `Eye`, `ENT`, `Cross`). Each becomes a filter chip. Chip order = key order in the file. `Cross` holds cross-cutting histories (e.g. "Smoking history"). Systems apply to `conditions.json` only.
- **Second-level key = condition name**, shown as the title and in the "Or pick one" list. Must be unique within a system. List order is by system (in file order), then condition (in file order).
- **`prompt`**: required string (usually "Symptoms to ask about" or "History to ask about"). Shown under the title; may be empty ("").
- **`examination`**: signs a clinician would look for or elicit on examination (general, system-specific, relevant special tests). Examination items often share terms with presentation items (e.g. "oedema"); a shared term ticks both, which is accepted.
- **Sections**: every key for the file must be present, shown in the order above; an empty one renders "None listed for this condition." A section is either a plain array of items (one unnamed category) or an object `{"Category": [items]}` whose key order is the category order (`lib/data.js` accepts both). Items are flattened in category order, and tiles are numbered 1..n within each section in that order. Empty categories are omitted. Every item sits in exactly one category.
- **Item `label`**: text revealed on the tile (and the answer shown on reveal). Rendered as plain React text (escaped), not HTML.
- **Item `keywords`**: a single string of `|`-separated alternatives, compiled by `rx()` in `lib/match.js` into a case-insensitive regex. Each alternative is a regex fragment (so `.`, `.*`, `.?` work, e.g. `"ex.?smok"`, `"wake.*breath"`), and is prefixed with `\b`.
  - Alternatives of 3 chars or fewer (after trimming trailing spaces) are matched as whole words (optional plural `s`), and during live typing must be followed by a delimiter, so `ed` doesn't fire while typing `edema`. Alternatives longer than 3 chars are prefix matches (`orthop` matches `orthopnoea`), so use stems.
  - A leading `~` matches anywhere instead of at a word start (drug suffixes, e.g. `~pril`). Alternatives starting with a non-word character are used as raw regex.
  - Don't use `(`, `)`, `[`, `]` or `\` unless you intend regex syntax; never put a literal `|` inside an alternative.
  - If several tiles match, the one whose match starts earliest, then is longest, wins (ties credit all). If the typed text is still a proper prefix of a longer literal keyword of another tile, found or not (e.g. `sputum` vs `sputum culture`), nothing fires while typing; after a 0.7s pause it credits a new tile, but never flashes yellow for an already-named one (Enter still does).
  - Abbreviations: `ABBR` in `lib/match.js` maps common abbreviations (htn, af, ckd, ...) to full terms. It is only tried when the typed text matches no tile, and while typing only after the 0.7s idle pause (so `ra` can't fire mid-`radiotherapy`). A keyword that already contains the abbreviation takes precedence. Add new entries to `ABBR`; avoid ambiguous ones (pe, mr, pd, ca, ed, us, cf).
  - Synonyms: `lib/synonyms.js` lists groups of interchangeable terms (erythema / redness, fever / pyrexia, kidney / renal, ...). Same fallback rules as `ABBR`: tried only when the typed text matches no tile, and while typing only after the idle pause. Whole words / phrases only; keep groups specific. Add a group there rather than widening individual tiles' keywords when the equivalence is general.
  - Where one word could name both a diagnosis and its test (iron, B12, electrolytes, urate), the diagnosis tile needs a qualifier (e.g. `iron deficiency`).

## Hint

Off by default; a "Hint" button beside Reveal (a checkbox toggle button, aria-pressed) toggles category boxes (`fieldset.cat` with a `legend`, wrapping flex, `.cats` in `recall-drill.css`) around the tiles of the current section only: the one Reveal names, or the open one when collapsed (`hintCode` from `useDrillGame`). Other sections stay flat, except that a complete section (every tile named, credited, ignored or revealed) always shows its category boxes, hint or not. The button is disabled when that section has no named categories and hidden once the round is over. `hint` lives in the round state (`s.hint`) as the code of the section it was switched on for; it only applies while `hintCode` still equals it, so completing that section (by typing or Reveal) or moving to the next hides it again and the next section isn't spoiled. It resets on every round/restart and is never saved. Tile index, number, matching, scoring, ignore and saved keys are unaffected.

## Category vocabulary (strict)

Every section in both JSON files is `{ "Category": [items] }`, and every category name must come from `categories.json` (per file, per section; list order = display order, each entry has a one-line `def` assignment rule). Categories appear only for entries that use them; single-item categories are allowed; there is no "Other". Items keep their original relative order within a category.

- To add or rename a category: edit `categories.json` first, then reassign items, then run `npm run check:categories` (it fails on any out-of-vocabulary or out-of-order category, empty category, or malformed item). It must pass before committing JSON changes.
- Conditions presentation also has **Acute on chronic** (just before Complications): features of an acute exacerbation / decompensation on top of the chronic disease (HF exacerbation, uraemia in CKD, COPD exacerbation, DKA, adrenal crisis). Conditions medications ends with **Avoid**: drugs, interventions and behaviours to avoid or use with caution (e.g. nitrates in aortic stenosis, NSAIDs in HF / CKD), named with the reason. Both are used only where they are a standard high-yield teaching point, not on every condition. Precipitants of decompensation stay under risks / Triggers.
- Conditions presentation includes the special cases Prodromal and Postdromal (used only where they fit); all neurological symptoms (motor, sensory, autonomic, bulbar) go under Neurological. Head and neck examination findings go to Neurological (eye, vision, hearing), Cardiorespiratory (JVP) or Local and peripheral signs (mouth, ENT, thyroid, nodes). Presentations differentials: when an item's mechanism is unambiguous (pneumonia, lung cancer, PE) use the mechanism category (Infective, Malignant, Vascular), otherwise the organ system. Presentations investigations share the conditions investigations list.
- Tool-assisted remapping (subagents): they may only use vocabulary names and must report items that fit nothing as vocabulary gaps rather than inventing categories.

## Editing guidelines

- Content lives in the database. Editing the JSON files changes nothing in the app (and `migrations/0003_seed_builtin.sql` is generated once and never regenerated, so ids stay stable). To change a built-in quiz use the quiz editor (or `PUT /api/quizzes/:id`).
- `npm run check:categories` still validates the JSON files' shape and category vocabulary; use it if you edit them for a future re-seed.
- When writing quiz `data` by hand keep it valid JSON; items have no ids or ordering requirement beyond position, and the section (and category) an item sits in is its identity.

## Code notes

- `lib/data.js` `drillFromQuiz(quiz)` turns a quiz from the API (`{id, owner, forked_from, data}`) into the game's drill `{key (the quiz id), mode, system, name, prompt, owner, forked_from, deleted, items:[{label, keywords, code, cat, key, re, lits}]}`, codes P/R/E/M/T (presentation, risks, examination, investigations, medications / treatment) or D/A/I (differentials, associated, investigations) or S (symptoms). `cat` is the category name or `null`. A `re` regex and literal keyword list (`lits`, for deferral) are attached to each item; `judge()` picks the tile(s). The API stores quiz data as sent, so malformed items (non-string label or keywords, an invalid regex) are skipped and an unknown mode gives no drill. Results are memoised per quiz object, so regexes compile once. `lib/content.js` loads and remembers API responses for the session.
- Section header icons are 16x16 pixel-art SVGs in `assets/` (`presentation.svg` thermometer, `risks.svg` warning triangle, `examination.svg` magnifying glass, `investigations.svg` flask, `medications.svg` pill), imported as URLs and drawn as a CSS `mask` filled with `--teal`, so they follow the theme. Each is one `currentColor` path of 1px rects with `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`; they render at 24px (1.5x the 16px heading text), which is sharp on 2x/3x screens but slightly uneven at 1x; 32px is the next exact integer scale.
- Expanding (the Expand toggle, or Reveal ending the round) scrolls the section just revealed or left open into view (`scrollTo` ref in `routes/Drill.jsx`; typing the last item does not scroll).
- Typography uses Apple system fonts with Inter as a fallback; no external requests.
- Colour variables are in `:root`, with light/dark via `prefers-color-scheme`.

## Accounts

Optional: sign in with Google (Better Auth, `worker/auth.js`); anyone with a Google account can sign up. The drill defaults to "Guest: nothing is saved" (ignoring a tile still works within the round). The header "Log in" link opens `/recall-drill/login` (`routes/Login.jsx`), a "Sign in with Google" button; new users land on `/recall-drill/profile` (`routes/Profile.jsx`) to choose an optional username (3-30 of `A-Za-z0-9_-`, unique, case-insensitive), otherwise the Google name is shown (`displayName()` in `lib/auth.js`). The header shows the Google avatar (`referrerPolicy="no-referrer"`) and the display name, which together with the arrow form one button; clicking it opens a dropdown with Profile and Log out (Escape, a click outside or navigating closes it). `/recall-drill/profile` is an old-macOS-style preferences pane (`.pref`): a title strip, then rows with the category heading in the left column (Account, Username, Session) and the content on the right; under 600px each row stacks. `/privacy` (`privacy.html`) is the privacy policy linked from the Google consent screen.

- `worker/app.js`: the host-agnostic API (Hono) taking `getAuth(c)` (a Better Auth instance), `getStore(c)` (`getHistory(userId)` / `putHistory(userId, rows)`) and an optional `limitWrites(c, key)`. `/api/auth/*` is Better Auth; `GET /api/history` returns the signed-in user's record and `PUT /api/history` replaces it (401 with no session, 403 for a cross-origin write, 429 over 60 saves a minute per user). The user is always the session's `users.id`, never a client claim. `worker/auth.js` builds the Better Auth instance per request (Workers expose bindings only per request): D1 binding `DB`, Google provider, `username` and `admin` plugins, UUID ids, database-backed rate limits. `worker/history.js` maps the client record to `history` rows and validates it; `worker/d1-store.js` is the D1 adapter; `worker/index.js` the Worker entry. Only `/api/*` and `/recall-drill/*` run the Worker (`run_worker_first` in `wrangler.jsonc`); everything else is static assets. To host elsewhere, mount `createApp({getAuth, getStore})` with another store adapter and serve `recall-drill/index.html` for unmatched `/recall-drill/*` paths.
- Storage is the D1 database bound as `DB`; the schema is in `migrations/` (applied with wrangler, no lazy table creation): Better Auth's `users`, `sessions`, `accounts`, `verifications`, `rateLimits`, and `history(user_id, quiz_id, data, updated)`, one row per user per drill (`data` is the drill record plus `starred`; a starred drill never played is `{"starred":true}`). `quiz_id` is the quiz's id (`migrations/0004_history_quiz_ids.sql` rewrote the old `mode|system|name` keys, stars included, to the built-in quiz ids; rows whose key matched no quiz were left alone and are ignored). The legacy username accounts became placeholder users (id = old username, email `<name>@legacy.invalid`); `scripts/claim-legacy.example.sql` moves one onto a real Google account.
- Secrets and vars: `BETTER_AUTH_URL` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` are `vars` in `wrangler.jsonc`; `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` are Worker secrets (`npx wrangler secret put <NAME>`). For local dev copy `.dev.vars.example` to `.dev.vars` (gitignored). Google redirect URI: `https://practicals.oyc.id.au/api/auth/callback/google`; only that origin can sign in (not `workers.dev` or preview URLs). Google's OAuth app is published to production; admins are set with `UPDATE users SET role='admin' WHERE id=...` in the D1 console.
- `lib/storage.js`: `fetchHistory`/`putHistory` and a localStorage hint (`recallDrillSignedIn`) so only likely-signed-in loads wait for the saved record; `AccountProvider` (`useSession()` from `lib/auth.js`) loads it whenever the session user changes (the drill route waits for that, as it needs the record).
- Record shape: `{v:1, stars:[quizId], drills:{quizId:{runs,last,score,found:["code:label"],credited:[...],ignored:[...]}}}`. Items are keyed by `sectionCode:label`, so renaming a label in a quiz orphans its saved state.
- `AccountContext` saves with a debounced whole-record `PUT` (`mutate()` then `save()`/`flush()`): on finishing a round, on credit toggles after finishing, on star and ignore toggles, and on Menu/Back.
- Ignored tiles (missed tiles and purple drill tiles offer the `−` bubble on hover or tap, and so do correct (typed or credited) tiles once their section is complete, i.e. every tile in it is named, credited, ignored or revealed; press it again to restore) stay visible but greyed, count as already named when typed, and are excluded from the score and counters. They are always prefilled on the next attempt.
- A drill with history shows a popover (on hover, or on tap) with "Start from scratch" (light green), "Redo incorrect" (light red) and "Reset progress" (plain). Redo incorrect navigates to the drill with route state `{inc:true}`: found and credited tiles from the saved record are prefilled, so only the blanks remain. Both options record the result: a redo round carries the saved found and credited forward, so the saved score is the combined score, and it counts as a run and updates `last`. Start from scratch begins blank (ignored tiles are still prefilled). On the done screen, logged-in users get an "Add to history" checkbox (ticked by default, unticked after Redo / Retry incorrect, so those rounds are not recorded unless the user ticks it; a ticked round is saved and `flush()`ed immediately). Unticking restores the drill's record to how it was before the round (`prevRec`: runs, last, score, found, credited) and re-ticking saves the round again, each flushed at once; while unticked, credit toggles don't touch the record (ignored tiles still save). The done screen also has "Retry incorrect" (hidden when nothing was missed): it restarts the drill with the tiles found or credited in the round just played prefilled, via `restart({found, credited})` (a new `useDrillGame` round), so it works for guests too and, for accounts, records like Redo incorrect. A drill with no history starts immediately when clicked. Logged-in users get a ★ button on each list row, shown on hover (always, dimmed, on touch devices) and kept visible when starred; it toggles the star without opening the drill. Logged-in users also get a History panel to the right of the list (below it on narrow screens): drills with `runs>0`, most recent first, showing score, system and date. Hover or tap a row for Restart / Redo incorrect / Delete (the same actions as the popover, via `DrillActions`; Delete is the same as Reset progress). "Reset progress" (confirm first) clears runs, score, found and credited for that drill; ignored items and the star are kept.

Local dev: `npm run dev` only; `@cloudflare/vite-plugin` runs the Worker and a local D1 (`.wrangler/`) inside the Vite dev server; apply the schema locally with `npx wrangler d1 migrations apply practicals --local` and put dummy values in `.dev.vars`. Google sign-in cannot complete on a cloud dev server (Google redirects to the browser's `localhost`); the real login is only testable on the deployed site. Production uses the D1 database whose id is in `wrangler.jsonc` (previews share it); keep the Worker name `practicals` to match the Workers Builds project. Production migrations are applied by hand: back up with `npx wrangler d1 export practicals --remote --output backup.sql`, then `npx wrangler d1 migrations apply practicals --remote`.

## Quizzes and playlists (API)

Users can own **quizzes** and **playlists** (ordered, sectioned lists of quiz references). Everything is readable by anyone, including guests; any signed-in user can edit a quiz or a playlist (`PUT`; only the owner can delete a playlist or soft-delete a quiz). The UI reads this API: Home shows the three built-in playlists, Playlist and Drill load from it, and History resolves its quizzes with `GET /api/quizzes?ids=...&full=1`. The playlist page has an Edit button for signed-in users (`components/PlaylistEditor.jsx`, @dnd-kit): title, description, sections (add, rename, reorder) and quiz rows dragged by their handle within or between sections. The quiz editor (`components/QuizEditor.jsx`, route `/q/<id>/edit`) opens from the pencil on the drill page, the Edit entry in the quiz popover (also opened by right-click) and the history panel; Manual tab: name, prompt and, per section, categories (hint column) with items (label plus comma-separated matches stored as `a|b|c`), a dotted "New item" gap between items and a delete button; the Agentic tab is a placeholder. There is no UI yet to create, fork or delete quizzes, or to create, copy, fork or delete playlists; the API for all of them exists. The API does no content validation: a body must be a JSON object and is stored as sent, except that the Worker owns a quiz's `deleted` flag.

- Tables (`migrations/0002_quizzes_playlists.sql`): `quizzes(id, owner_id, forked_from, data)` and `playlists(...)`, the same columns. Ids are UUIDv7 strings (`uuid`), so `ORDER BY id DESC` is newest first and there is no `created_at`. `owner_id` references `users(id)` with `ON DELETE RESTRICT` (a user who owns content can't be deleted; Better Auth's user deletion is off); `forked_from` is `ON DELETE SET NULL`. D1 enforces foreign keys.
- `quizzes.data` is the drill (`{mode, system, name, prompt, <section keys>, deleted}`, sections as in "JSON format" above; `mode` is `cond`, `pres` or `sys`), so `lib/data.js` flattening and the item keys (`code:label`) work unchanged. A quiz's legacy history key is `mode|system|name`. `playlists.data` is `{title, description, sections:[{title, quiz_ids:[quizId]}]}`; a quiz id should appear once per playlist.
- Built-ins (`migrations/0003_seed_builtin.sql`, generated once by `node scripts/seed-builtins.mjs`, which refuses to overwrite it so ids stay stable): the 187 drills as quizzes and three playlists (Conditions, one section per system; Presentations; Systems review), owned by the placeholder user `ollie` (`scripts/claim-legacy.example.sql` hands ownership to a real account). Editing `conditions.json` etc. does not change these rows.
- Routes (`worker/quizzes.js`, `worker/playlists.js`, shared helpers in `worker/content-util.js`; all JSON). Writes need a session and a same-origin request (401 / 403); every UPDATE and DELETE includes `AND owner_id = ?` and returns 404 when it changes nothing, so "missing" and "not yours" look the same.
  - `GET /api/quizzes`, `GET /api/playlists`: summaries (quizzes: `mode/system/name/prompt/deleted`; playlists: `title/description/sectionCount`) with `owner: {id, name, image}` (never email), newest first, `?owner=me|<id>`, `?before=<id>&limit=<n>` (max 100) paging via `next`. Deleted quizzes are hidden unless you ask for your own with `?owner=me&deleted=1`. More filters: `?ids=<id,id,...>` (up to 100, with `&full=1` to include each quiz's `data`), `?mode=`, `?system=`, `?name=` (exact). Public reads (everything except `owner=me`) carry an ETag and `cache-control: no-cache`, so an unchanged playlist revalidates with a 304; all other responses are `no-store`.
  - `GET /api/quizzes/:id` (deleted ones too, with `data.deleted`), `POST /api/quizzes` (body = the data), `PUT /api/quizzes/:id` (any signed-in user; body validated by `validQuiz` in `worker/content-util.js`; replaces the data, keeps `deleted`; every playlist that references the quiz sees it), `PUT /api/quizzes/:id/deleted` (`{"deleted":true|false}`; playlists are untouched, so restoring brings it back everywhere), `POST /api/quizzes/:id/fork` (your own copy, `forked_from` set, not deleted).
  - `GET /api/playlists/:id`: the playlist with its quizzes grouped by section in order, deleted quizzes included with their flag; `?quizzes=meta` leaves the quiz bodies out (the Conditions playlist is about 320 KB in full, 20 KB as meta). `POST /api/playlists`, `PUT /api/playlists/:id` (replaces the data; any signed-in user, body validated as `{title, description, sections:[{title, quiz_ids}]}`), `DELETE /api/playlists/:id` (hard delete, quizzes untouched), `POST /api/playlists/:id/copy` (same sections and quiz ids, quizzes not copied).
  - `POST /api/playlists/:pid/quizzes/:qid/fork`: fork a quiz and swap the fork into your own playlist at the same place, in one `db.batch()` (the copy is inserted only if you own the playlist, and the playlist is updated only if the copy exists, so neither half can happen alone). Returns the new quiz.
- No quotas, size limits or moderation endpoints exist yet; D1's 2 MB row limit is the only cap.
